Questions & Answers

Can I wear the Tulsi Mala while bathing?

Yes! According to the Skanda Purana, bathing while wearing Tulsi wood grants the immense spiritual benefit of bathing in all holy rivers (tirthas). Water passing over sacred Tulsi purifies both physical and subtle bodies.

Where are these malas crafted?

Each mala is hand-carved specifically at Radha Kund (Vraja Dham) by local traditional Vaisnava artisans.

How to verify authenticity?

Genuine Holy Basil wood carries a light herbal scent and floats in water.
