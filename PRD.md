# Project Requirements Document (PRD) - Tulsi Shop

## 1. Overview
Tulsi Shop is a specialized, devotional e-commerce platform offering hand-carved Tulsi Kanthi Malas, bracelets, and spiritual items crafted by Vaisnava artisans at Radha Kund (Vraja Dham).

## 2. Target Audience
Devotees, practitioners of Sanatana Dharma, and consumers looking for authentic, scripture-based, ethically sourced Radha Kund Tulsi products.

## 3. Core Features
- **Hero & Storytelling Banner:** Deep devotional branding highlighting sacred origins.
- **Scripture References (Puranas):** Dedicated sections highlighting verses from Padma Purana and Skanda Purana detailing the spiritual benefits of wearing Tulsi.
- **Product Catalog Grid:** Displaying products with high-resolution photography, descriptions, categories, ratings, and pricing.
- **Direct WhatsApp Ordering:** Instant order placement via direct WhatsApp redirection.
- **Customer Engagement:** Interactive Devotee Reviews section and a public Q&A tab for care/bathing guidelines.
- **Side Drawer Navigation:** Slide-out menu containing quick links, wishlist counts, and origin notices.
